# Voice-first video prompt — template

One prompt per part. Parts split at sentence ends, each at most the model's ceiling.
Repeat the STYLE and WORLD blocks verbatim in every part. Fill only the beats.

---

STYLE. <Medium named first, in production language: "hand-drawn 2D animation in a flat
screenprint style" / "stop-motion style 3D with a handcrafted felt-and-wood look".>
<Ground colour. Fill colours. Line colour and weight.> <Each accent colour and the one
thing it is reserved for.> <Mood words the palette must obey: bright, light, not moody.>
<Forbidden: gradients, 3D, photographic look, lettering, whatever the medium excludes.>
<Timing idiom if 2D: animated on twos with held frames.> <Part length> vertical film in
<N> shots.

WORLD. <The metaphor in one sentence.> <Each recurring object named once with what it
stands for.> <Who may appear, and where they may not.> <If this part continues another:
"It continues directly from a previous part that ended on <observed end state>.">

The film is silent; a narrator will be laid over it afterwards. Each shot gives its
time, the line the narrator speaks during it, and what the picture must make clear.
Cut only where marked. Inside a shot, stay in one place. Choose the staging, the
actions and the movement yourself.

Shot 1, <t0> to <t1> seconds. Narrator: "<exact voice line(s) spoken in this window>."
Picture: <one sentence: what must be legible to a cold viewer during these words>.

Cut.

Shot 2, <t1> to <t2> seconds. Narrator: "<line>." Picture: <one sentence>.

Cut.

<…>

---

Do not add: hand gestures, object handling steps, camera moves, lens or depth-of-field
words, brand or franchise names, on-screen text. If a beat fails, retake that part with
one added sentence of direction for that beat only.
